## How the Digestive System Works

The digestive system is the pathway that breaks down food, absorbs nutrients and removes waste.

Digestive system function depends on muscles that move food along, enzymes that break it down and trillions of gut microbes that help with fermentation and immune signals.

A Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan article says your liver, kidneys and digestive tract already filter and eliminate waste through regular bowel movements without outside intervention.

Understanding this process can help you make better choices about [how to improve digestion ](https://bakrus-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/what-are-the-best-natural-ways-to-improve-digestion)naturally through healthy eating habits, hydration, and everyday lifestyle changes.

That is a useful reminder when someone sells you a way to "fix" what the body already does.

## How to Help Digestion Every Day

For how to speed up digestion, how to improve digestion naturally at home and easy digestion, a Scripps Health article by Kaylan Graham, MD,[ six tips to prevent digestive problems](https://www.scripps.org/news_items/5225-six-tips-to-prevent-digestive-problems), displayed April 22, 2025, is a practical start:

1. Eat smaller, more frequent meals: four to five small meals instead of three, without eating more overall, and chew slowly.
2. Drink plenty of water and limit alcohol: water helps the colon work and prevents constipation, while alcohol can cause heartburn and diarrhea.
3. Exercise regularly: walking, cycling, swimming or hiking helps the intestines move waste.
4. Maintain a healthy weight: extra weight, especially around the midsection, can worsen heartburn, gas and burping.
5. Eat a balanced diet: avoid fried, greasy foods and favor fiber-rich beans, whole grains and nuts.
6. Manage stress: stress affects digestion through the brain-gut connection, so try relaxation and enough sleep.

Dr. Graham says digestive problems are often not serious, but if they do not go away, contact your doctor.

## Gut Healthy Foods: What the Research Suggests

If you want the best foods for gut health, the foods for gut health with the strongest story are fermented foods and fiber-rich plants.

A Stanford trial reported by[ FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2021/11/15/Study-Diet-rich-in-fermented-foods-can-increase-gut-microbiome-diversity-and-immune-response/) on November 15, 2021, assigned 36 healthy adults to one of two 10-week diets, one rich in fermented foods and one rich in fiber.

The fermented group ate foods such as yogurt, kefir, cottage cheese, kimchi, other fermented vegetables and kombucha.

Their overall microbial diversity increased, larger servings had stronger effects, and 19 inflammatory proteins in the blood decreased.

The high-fiber group, which ate legumes, seeds, whole grains, nuts, vegetables and fruit, showed a less pronounced effect on diversity in that short period.

That does not mean fiber is unimportant.

The researchers suggested a longer study might be needed. Here is a simple list of gut healthy foods and gut health foods based on the sources above:

* Fermented foods: yogurt, kefir, kimchi, fermented vegetables, kombucha
* Fiber-rich plants: beans, lentils, whole grains, vegetables, fruit, nuts and seeds
* Omega-3 fish: Scripps notes it may reduce inflammation
* Probiotic dairy: yogurt and kefir, per Scripps

These are foods good for gut health in the sense that research or an expert source links them to gut balance.

This was a small trial, so treat it as a promising lead.

Introduce new foods gradually, since a sudden jump in fiber or fermented foods can cause gas.

## Gut Cleanse, Reset and Heal Your Gut: What's Real?

## When to See a Doctor

See a doctor if you have trouble swallowing, bloody or black vomit or stool, severe or ongoing stomach pain, sudden weight loss, a persistent change in bowel habits or symptoms that don't go away.

Scripps lists several of these as reasons for immediate care.

People with a family history of bowel disease or cancer should ask about screening.
